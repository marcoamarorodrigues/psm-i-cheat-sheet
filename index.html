03Scrum Team· typically ≤ 10 people · cross-functional · self-managing · no sub-teams, no hierarchies

Product OwnerAccountable for maximizing product value & effective Product Backlog management. One person, not a committee.
Scrum MasterAccountable for the Scrum Team's effectiveness. A true leader who serves the team & the organization.
DevelopersCreate the Sprint plan, instill quality via the DoD, adapt the plan daily, hold each other accountable.

04Events· max timeboxes for a 1-month Sprint (shorter Sprints → usually shorter)

SPRINT · fixed length ≤ 1 month · the container
8h
Sprint Planning
Why is it valuable? · What can be Done? · How will it get done?
15 min
Daily Scrum
Developers inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal.
4h
Sprint Review
Inspect outcome, adapt Product Backlog. A working session — never a gate.
3h
Retrospective
Plan ways to increase quality & effectiveness. Concludes the Sprint.
Next Sprint starts immediately after the previous oneOnly the PO can cancel — if the Sprint Goal becomes obsolete

!Exam traps — what the Guide actually says

✓It's accountabilities, not roles. "Increment Goal" doesn't exist.
✓SM is not a project manager & doesn't assign work.
✓PO may delegate backlog work, but stays accountable.
✓Scope may be renegotiated with the PO; quality never decreases.
✓Many Increments per Sprint; release before the Sprint ends is OK.
✓Not Done? Can't be released or shown at Review → back to PB.
✓Many teams, one product: 1 PB, 1 PO, 1 Product Goal, shared DoD.
✓PO/SM attend the Daily only if they work on Sprint Backlog items (as Developers).
