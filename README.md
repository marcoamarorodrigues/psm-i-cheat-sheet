## 📌 About

Preparing for the **PSM I** usually means reading the Scrum Guide several times and searching for cheat sheets online. The problem: many of those cheat sheets are based on older versions of the Guide or contain errors that can cost points in the exam.

This project is a **single, responsive HTML page** that condenses the Scrum Guide 2020 into a visual reference you can open on your phone before the exam, print as an A4 page, or keep open while studying. Every statement was checked against the official text.

## 🧭 What's inside

| # | Section | What it covers |
|:-:|---|---|
| 01 | **Foundations** | What Scrum is · empiricism & lean thinking · the 3 pillars (Transparency, Inspection, Adaptation) · the 5 values (Commitment, Focus, Openness, Respect, Courage) |
| 02 | **Artifacts & Commitments** | Product Backlog → **Product Goal** · Sprint Backlog → **Sprint Goal** · Increment → **Definition of Done** · refinement as an ongoing activity |
| 03 | **Scrum Team** | Team size, cross-functionality, self-management and the accountabilities of the **Product Owner**, **Scrum Master** and **Developers** |
| 04 | **Events** | The Sprint as a container and the maximum timeboxes for a 1-month Sprint |
| ⚠️ | **Exam traps** | 8 common misconceptions, with what the Guide actually says |
| 🎯 | **Exam facts** | Format of the assessment and how to approach the questions |

### ⏱️ Timeboxes at a glance (1-month Sprint)

| Event | Max timebox | Purpose |
|---|:-:|---|
| Sprint | ≤ 1 month | Container for all other events |
| Sprint Planning | 8 h | Why · What · How |
| Daily Scrum | 15 min | Inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal |
| Sprint Review | 4 h | Inspect the outcome, adapt the Product Backlog |
| Sprint Retrospective | 3 h | Improve quality and effectiveness |

## ✅ What makes it different

Common mistakes found in other cheat sheets, corrected here:

| ❌ Often seen | ✅ Scrum Guide 2020 |
|---|---|
| "Roles" | **Accountabilities** |
| "Increment Goal" | Doesn't exist. The 3 commitments are Product Goal, Sprint Goal and Definition of Done |
| "Servant leader" | "A true leader who serves the Scrum Team and the larger organization" |
| "Only the PO can change the Product Backlog" | The PO may delegate the work, but remains accountable |
| "The Increment is only delivered at the Sprint Review" | Multiple Increments can be created and released before the Sprint ends |

## 🚀 How to use

- **Online:** open the <a href="https://marcoamarorodrigues.github.io/psm-i-cheat-sheet/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">live page</a>. It adapts to mobile, tablet and desktop.
- **Print:** in the browser, *Print → Save as PDF* (A4, background graphics on).
- **Offline:** download `index.html`. It is a single, self-contained file with no dependencies.

> 💡 **Study tip:** PSM I questions are scenario-based. Don't memorize rules. Think *empiricism, self-management and value*, and watch for keywords like **best**, **most likely**, **should** and **must**.
